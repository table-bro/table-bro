# Interface: BroConfigCSS

The classes of the table and of its shadows.

## Properties

### shadow?

> `optional` **shadow?**: `object`

The shadows of the toolbar, the header, the body and the paginator.

#### className?

> `optional` **className?**: `string`

The class of the shadows.

***

### table?

> `optional` **table?**: `object`

The tables of the header and of the body.

#### className?

> `optional` **className?**: `string`

The class of the tables.

#### tbody?

> `optional` **tbody?**: `object`

The body row group.

##### tbody.className?

> `optional` **className?**: `string`

The class of the body row group.

##### tbody.tr?

> `optional` **tr?**: `object`

A body row.

##### tbody.tr.className?

> `optional` **className?**: `string`

The class of a body row.

##### tbody.tr.td?

> `optional` **td?**: `object`

A body cell.

##### tbody.tr.td.className?

> `optional` **className?**: `string`

The class of a body cell.

> `optional` **thead?**: `object`

The header row group.

##### thead.className?
